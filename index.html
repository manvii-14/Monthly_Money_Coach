💸 Monthly Money Coach

How to use: When prompted for "Input", use the guide below.

  • 1 Add Expense (Allowed Categories: Food, Transport, Bills, Entertainment, Other)
  • 2 View All (Displays all saved expenses)
  • 3 Search (Find an expense by Date or Category)
  • 4 Reports (View Monthly Total, Budget Warning, and Category Breakdown)
  • 5 Exit (Stops the program loop)
